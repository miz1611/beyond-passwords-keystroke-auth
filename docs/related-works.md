# Related Works

Annotated bibliography of the sources cited in `BEYOND_PASSWORDS.pdf`,
grouped by theme.

## Keystroke dynamics & behavioural biometrics (technical foundation)

1. **R. Dillon and Arushi**, "An agent-based modelling approach to free-text
   keyboard dynamics for continuous authentication," May 2024.
   https://www.researchgate.net/publication/380877463
   — Free-text continuous authentication approach; informed the choice of
   digraph-level (local) features over simple average typing speed.

2. **A. Martins, T. Dias, A. Dias, J. Vitorino, E. Maia, I. Praça**,
   "Keystroke dynamics for intelligent biometric authentication with
   machine learning," Jan. 2025. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42452-025-07449-5
   — ML-based keystroke authentication; benchmark reference for
   accuracy/EER comparison against Random Forest.

7. **V. Dhakal, A. Feit, P. Kristensson, A. Oulasvirta**, "Observations on
   Typing from 136 Million Keystrokes," May 2018.
   https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174220
   — Large-scale typing behaviour dataset/observations; grounds the
   dwell-time / flight-time feature choices in real-world typing data.
